# Aatmunn

> Aatmunn (formerly Guardhat, founded 2014 in Detroit, headquartered in Livonia, Michigan) is an industrial safety and asset intelligence platform for frontline and deskless workers. Its SPANR / Industrial Internet of People (IIoP) platform unifies workers, workplaces, and assets into one real-time system with AI-driven insights for predictive hazard detection, emergency response, inspections, and compliance. Aatmunn publishes no public developer API, SDKs, or OpenAPI at this time.
